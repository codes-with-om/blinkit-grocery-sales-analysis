# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing BlinkIT grocery sales data using Microsoft Excel to uncover meaningful business insights, evaluate outlet performance, and understand sales patterns across different product categories.

The analysis follows a business-driven approach, addressing key business questions through data cleaning, statistical analysis, PivotTables, and interactive dashboards.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project addresses four major business objectives:

### Objective 1: Overall Sales Performance
- Analyse overall sales performance.
- Evaluate key business metrics and KPIs.
- Understand sales distribution across different dimensions.

### Objective 2: Product & Outlet Analysis
- Compare product and outlet performance.
- Identify sales patterns across different business segments.
- Evaluate key contributors to overall sales.

### Objective 3: Outlet Performance & Sales Relationships
- Compare Low Fat and Regular product sales across outlets.
- Analyse the relationship between item visibility and sales.
- Evaluate product category sales contributions across outlet types.

### Objective 4: Statistical Analysis of Sales
- Calculate Average, Median, Minimum, and Maximum Sales.
- Measure sales variation using Standard Deviation.
- Identify unusual sales values using the Interquartile Range (IQR) method.
- Compare average sales across product categories, outlet types, and outlet locations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Excel Formulas & Functions
- Slicers & Interactive Dashboards
- Statistical Analysis (IQR, Quartiles, Standard Deviation)

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and prepared the raw dataset using Power Query.
2. **Data Analysis:** Used Excel formulas and PivotTables to analyse sales performance.
3. **Statistical Analysis:** Evaluated sales distribution, variation, and potential outliers.
4. **Data Visualization:** Developed interactive dashboards using PivotCharts and slicers.
5. **Business Insights:** Interpreted analytical findings to support business decision-making.
